description: Catch up on what the team shipped and where things stand
argument-hint: "[hours, default 24]"
---
Call `batcave_pulse` (hours: $ARGUMENTS if it is a number, otherwise 24).

Turn it into a short briefing, at most six lines: what shipped, what is waiting on the user, what is running, stale claims, new decisions. End with the single most useful next step for the user.
